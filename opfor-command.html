How to read it. Each order is split into the two phases of the turn. The bold line in each block is the instruction, and the bullets under it are qualifiers on how to carry it out, not separate steps. The ladder shows what that exact variant does at each range band, and the order follows the ladder.

Weapons. In Classic, each card shows the full loadout and heat sink count, so you know exactly what to roll on the record sheet without needing to know every variant by heart.

Tracking. In Alpha Strike, tap the armour and structure pips and the condition updates itself. In Classic, your record sheet holds the hit locations, so just set the condition. A crippled unit gets ordered off the field either way.
