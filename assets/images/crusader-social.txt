===== X_long =====

There are four old Star League heavies I keep coming back to: the Warhammer, the Marauder, the Thunderbolt and this one. We've covered the other three already.

The Crusader finishes the set.

It never had one trick that made people point and say that's why we need this thing. Wasn't the fastest, didn't hit hardest. It just kept being useful, whatever was on the order sheet that week, without getting dragged back into the bay for a rebuild.

And the forearms tell you almost everything about how it ended up looking the way it does. Early machines were putting too much stress through the arm actuators, so the engineers bolted on barrel-shaped collars to take the strain. The fix worked, so it stayed. Then it stayed for centuries. If the thing has forearms like Popeye, it's probably a Crusader.
