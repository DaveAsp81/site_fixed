Unit data

Battle Value, Point Value, introduction dates, weapon loadouts and per-era faction availability are reconciled against the Master Unit List, the official online unit database. Alpha Strike values are the standard card figures.

Historical and design background on the mech guides is drawn from Sarna.net, the BattleTech wiki, and is written up in our own words rather than reproduced. Sarna is community-maintained and worth supporting if you get use out of it.
