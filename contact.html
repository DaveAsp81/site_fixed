Found a bug in one of the tools?

Feedback from people actually playing games has fixed more problems here than anything else, so it is genuinely welcome. The more of this you can tell me, the faster it gets fixed:

  • Which tool, and what you were doing at the time
  • The unit or units involved, if it is a data problem
  • What you expected to happen, and what actually happened
  • A screenshot, if it is easier than describing it

If a unit has the wrong stats, telling me the exact chassis and variant helps a lot. Most of the data comes from the Master Unit List, so I can check it against the source.

Suggesting a feature

Happy to hear ideas. The tools are built around solo and co-op play, so the things most likely to get built are the ones that help when you are sitting down to a game without an opponent.
