Paint Schemes

Pick a unit and you get its primary, secondary and accent colours matched to a real pot in three ranges, plus the five steps to put them on a model. There is no colour picker here on purpose: the question a painter actually has is which pots do I buy, and in what order do I use them, and that is answerable from a table.

Paint hexes are approximations used for matching, not manufacturer specifications — paint on plastic under a lamp never matches a backlit screen. Treat a match as a starting point and check it against a real pot or a printed swatch card.

Schemes marked as an interpretation have not been checked against a sourcebook — the colours are a reasonable reading of general canon, not a citation. If you have the plate for one of them and the colours are wrong, tell me and I will fix the data: the whole list lives in one JSON file.
